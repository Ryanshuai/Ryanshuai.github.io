Vehicle Perception System

End-to-end vehicle perception pipeline deployed at scale across 1100+ sites

Object Detection Multi-Object Tracking License Plate OCR Vehicle Re-ID Edge Deployment

Overview

Designed and built a comprehensive vehicle perception system that integrates multiple computer vision modules into a unified pipeline. The system processes real-time video feeds to detect, track, and identify vehicles with high accuracy and low latency.

The pipeline handles the full lifecycle: from initial vehicle detection to persistent tracking across frames, license plate recognition, and cross-camera re-identification — enabling real-time traffic analytics at scale.
