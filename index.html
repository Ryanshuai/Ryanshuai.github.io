About Me

As a tech lead at a $5 billion AI company, I specialize in building and deploying computer vision systems at scale. With experience managing intelligent perception solutions across 1100+ sites, I bring a unique combination of technical depth and operational excellence to every project. My work spans the full spectrum of visual intelligence, from classical 2D computer vision algorithms to cutting-edge 3D reconstruction and spatial understanding.

I'm passionate about bridging the gap between research and production, transforming advanced CV and 3D vision techniques into robust, real-world applications. Whether it's developing deep learning models for vehicle perception, creating photorealistic 3D digital twins, or optimizing vision pipelines for edge deployment, I thrive on solving complex technical challenges that make a measurable impact. My approach combines strong fundamentals in mathematics and computer science with hands-on experience in modern ML frameworks and GPU computing.
